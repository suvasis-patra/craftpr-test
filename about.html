Our story

It started with a
simple problem.

In 2024, our founders were frustrated with the complexity of modern project management. Tools were either too powerful and overwhelming, or too simple and lacking. They knew there had to be a better way.

🎯

The problem

Teams were drowning in notifications, switching between dozens of tools, and losing precious time to administrative overhead. Work had become about managing work, not doing it.

💡

The insight

Great teams don't need more features—they need better workflows. The solution wasn't adding complexity, but removing it through intelligent automation and thoughtful design.

🚀

The solution

Flowly was born: a platform that brings everything together in one place, automates the repetitive stuff, and lets teams focus on what they do best—building amazing products.
